## General Information
Polar, compared to other anticheats, bundles many different checks under a category. That means that if someone is flagging for suspicious game protocol, that doesn't mean a specific cheat, but rather that the player their client is not following the vanilla protocol, which is often caused by cheats, or poorly written mods. The same goes for any alert shown for that matter.

## Flags

### Suspicious game protocol

As already mentioned in the general information section, suspicious game protocol flags because the player his client is not following the vanilla minecraft protocol while performing actions. This can be caused by many things, and doesn't necessarily mean the player is cheating, as unfortunately in the crystal pvp community we are dealing with many players that have tens if not hundreds of different mods installed, where most of them are written by 12 year olds that have no idea what they are doing. When a player makes an appeal and they have only been flagging this check, ask them for their mod folder, so we can determine what mod could be causing this. Generally speaking you may also unban this player, but do take a look at his previous punishment history. This check picks up many known cheaters, and if they do have a punishment history (by staff members or totemguard banning them), keep them banned.
