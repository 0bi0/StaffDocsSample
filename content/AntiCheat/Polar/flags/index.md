## General Information
Polar, compared to other anticheats, bundles many different checks under a category. That means that if someone is flagging for suspicious game protocol, that doesn't mean a specific cheat, but rather that the player their client is not following the vanilla protocol, which is often caused by cheats, or poorly written mods. The same goes for any alert shown for that matter.
