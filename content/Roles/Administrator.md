---
title: 3. Administrator
---
> ℹ️  An Administrator's main responsibilities include handling the staff team, and other important matters that do not yet require the attention of a [[System-Administrator]] or [[Owner]]. They are also apart of the [[Management]] team.
> 
> - This is the highest obtainable staff rank through promotions.

#### Administrators may independently exercise any of the following discretions:
- Perform rank transfers between two accounts ([[Rank Transfers]])
- Handle any doxx-related report tickets ([[Doxx Reports]])
- Issue warnings to staff members of a lower role
- Assign [[Mentor]]s for new staff members
