---
title: 6. Support
---
> [!note]
> The Support role is a trial phase where Management assesses whether an applicant is a good fit for the team - you can be either promoted or demoted during this phase, depending on how you perform.

> ℹ️ Their main responsibilities include assisting players with general inquiries, handling basic tickets, and redirecting issues to the appropriate staff members when required.
> 
> - This is the entry-level staff role.
