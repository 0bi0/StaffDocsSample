---
title: 1. Management
---
> ℹ️ Consists of [[Owner]], [[System-Administrator]]s, and [[Administrator]]s. 
> They are responsible for overseeing the server and addressing any issues or tasks that fall outside the duties of regular staff members.

This group is responsible for all major decisions, including server hosting, version upgrades, and planning upcoming updates or changes.

Sometimes referred to as an **Upper Management**.
