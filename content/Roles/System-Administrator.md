title: 2. System-Administrator
---
> ℹ️**System Administrators** hold the same level of permissions and authority as the **[[Owner]]**. This group consists of trusted System Administrators and Developers.
> 
> This position cannot be applied for, nor is it obtainable through regular staff promotions. Members are selected based on long-term trust, proven collaboration on past projects, or being close, technically experienced friends of the Owner.

### ☑️ Responsibilities

- Server Infrastructure
- Development on in house applications / plugins
- Bug Reports

### 📝 Note
The combined group of the [[Owner]] and System Administrators is sometimes internally referred to as **Upper Management**. This group is responsible for all major decisions, including server hosting, version upgrades, and planning upcoming updates or changes.
