---
title: Etiquette & Behaviour
---
We expect all of our staff members to uphold a **professional** image while handling scenarios.

Please do remember that **your behaviour** **reflects** how people view the server, and your fellow staff members.

> ### 🤬 For Example
>
>1. You say a slur in another server.
>2. A user sees this, then joins the server, and sees you as staff.
>3. User may feel uncomfortable playing, feeling the server may **reflect what you said**.
>
> <sub>⚠️ **Users may also open reports against your staff position.**</sub>
> 

We generally ask our staff to operate off **common sense**, while **remaining considerate** of others around them (i.e., other staff members & players).

**Insulting or bullying** players or fellow team members will not be tolerated.
You shouldn't be having fun at someone else's expense.
