>[!warning]
>Confirmed abuse of power **will** result in either a demotion or removal from the team.

## Professionalism & Staff Abuse Guidelines

Staff members represent the server and are expected to act professionally at all times. All staff members' actions must be fair, unbiased, and follow documented procedures. Abuse of staff privileges or unprofessional conduct will be taken seriously.

## Professional Conduct

All staff must:
- Treat players and other staff with respect
- Remain  professional in all situations
- Act based on evidence and documented policy
- Avoid public arguments or generally hostile behavior

> Remember: As staff you represent not only yourself, but also the server as a whole.

## Abuse of Power

Staff abuse includes, but is not limited to:
- Punishing without valid evidence
- Favouritism or selective enforcement
- Using staff tools for personal benefit
- Unauthorized trolling (see note below)

Note: Trolling **occasionally** is permitted, as long as a member of [[Management]] initiated the whole ordeal.
