---
title: Etiquette & Behaviour
---
We expect all of our staff members to uphold a **professional** image while handling scenarios.

Please do remember that **your behaviour** **reflects** how people view the server, and your fellow staff members.

> ### 🤬 For Example
>
>You say a slur in another server.
>A user sees this, then joins the server, and sees you as staff.
>User may feel uncomfortable playing, feeling the server may reflect what you said.

We generally ask our staff to operate off **common sense**, while **remaining considerate** of others around them (i.e., other staff members & players).

**Insulting or bullying** players or fellow team members will not be tolerated. Fun should not be had with your staff member privileges at someone else's expense.
