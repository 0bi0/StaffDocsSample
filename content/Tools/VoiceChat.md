> [!info]  
> Staff should **never use voice chat to act unprofessional or rage-bait players**, and all standard professionalism rules still apply while using simple voice chat.

We use **Simple Voice Chat** to let players talk to others near them in-game, making interactions feel more natural.


### 🎙️ Voice Chat Usage

> - `V` (default keybind)
>     
> 
> Push-to-talk key used to speak in proximity voice chat.  
> Can be changed in **Controls → Simple Voice Chat**.
> 
> - `/voicechat`
>     
> 
> Opens the Simple Voice Chat settings menu,  
> allowing you to adjust input/output devices and volume levels.
