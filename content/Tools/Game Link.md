title: Game Link
---
We provide staff with a **Discord** channel allowing them to perform in-game actions.

Located under **Staff Logs & Utilities**, you can type commands to lookup users' history and perform punishments **without the need of logging on**.

## Example usages

🔨 `Punishments`
```
MattMX: ban MattMX test

→ BAN » GameLink permanently banned MattMX for 'test' (120575 - Prac-1)
```

📕 `History Command`
```
MattMX: history MattMX

→ HISTORY » 10 Punishment History for MattMX
→ 
→ 14 days, 1 hour, 59 minutes ago
 1etho was banned by MattMX: 'Administrative Ban'
...
```
