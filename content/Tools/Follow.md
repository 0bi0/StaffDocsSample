---
title: Follow
---
We allow [[Support]] and upwards access to `/follow`, which will allow you to follow a player through practice and other servers without having to manually re-teleport to them.

Players often queue in `/1v1` and move around the server very fast - this provides an easy way to stay on their trail if you suspect them of cheating or something.

### ⌨️ Command Syntax

> - `/follow <username>` 
> 
> Starts following a username.
> Will automatically enable **Vanish**.
> 
> 
> - `/follow stop`
> 
> Stops following the current user.
